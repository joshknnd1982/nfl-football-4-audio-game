NFL Football 4 by Josh Kennedy, inspired by Jim Kitchen
This is a TEST build for the owner (version 0.12.0, the fourth), not a release.

This is inspired by Jim Kitchen. Jim Kitchen was blind from birth and made many free games for the blind community. He died in 2015. This game is loosely inspired by his "NFL football version 4": it is new code written from a description of how his program behaves, plays his original sound files, and adds the rules and plays of the real National Football League, the real head coaches and players of the 32 teams, time outs, penalties, replay challenges, quarterback runs, onside kicks and a Franchise mode. Version 0.10.0 added a radio-style broadcast (the announcer's banter and a color commentator in a voice of their own), stadium sounds (a crowd, hits, the first down chain), a referee who announces over a microphone, and the choice of the receiver of a pass (R) and of the player who blitzes (L). Version 0.11.0 adds: the color commentator's name (Ghip, a well-known sports podcaster from a list, or a name you type: F9, Rules, Commentator); your own sound files for the stadium, the referee's microphone and the whistles (F9, Sounds, Stadium and referee sounds); the game finds NVDA or JAWS by itself and asks once whether to speak through it; More Plays on defense lists every defensive call and the play menus say once where the other plays are; and the F5 and F6 key lists name R and L. Version 0.12.0 adds: the W key in the play menus, which says which play the playbook would most likely pick for the play type or the list you are on, and how often; the keys of the play menus (Enter on a play type, Right, M, D and W) each have their own line in F5 and F6; the Franchise plays the real 2026 schedule with bye weeks; the Washington team is the Commanders; and in the Franchise you can sign free agents during the season and trade until the deadline. The exact original is still there: choose Classic in the Rules setting (F9). The sound files are included on the maintainer's own responsibility: Jim Kitchen gave Josh Kennedy permission to use his wav files, but the original email is lost. A rights holder can ask for the files to be removed and they will be.

The N. F. L. names, the playbook ideas and the Franchise ideas are used descriptively. The game is not endorsed by the National Football League, its teams or Electronic Arts. Madden NFL is a trademark of Electronic Arts; the game only uses football plays as ideas and copies nothing from it.

How to play
- Start the game. It says "Welcome to Josh Kennedy's N. F. L. football, loosely inspired by Jim Kitchen's original games." Then it asks what to do about a saved game or season, if there is one.
- Up and Down move in a menu, the digits and letters choose, Enter confirms, F1 repeats the prompt.
- F5 lists the special keys, F6 explains the game, F7 gives the football words, F9 opens the added functions: speech settings, sounds, names and banter, rules and about.
- The T key calls a time out at any moment of a game. D says what a play does. R in the offense menu chooses who your next pass goes to; L in the defense menu chooses who blitzes (Enter on a play keeps the random pick).
- The new sounds are generated, not recordings: you can replace any of them with your own wav, ogg, flac or mp3 file (F9, Sounds, Stadium and referee sounds lists them with the file name each takes; docs\CUSTOMIZE.md). F9, Rules switches the commentator and the referee (Broadcast) and the stadium sounds off, and chooses the commentator's name.

Speech
The game finds NVDA or JAWS by itself. If one is running it asks once whether the game should speak through it (Enter or Y for yes, N for no, which means the game uses its own Windows voices); otherwise it uses a Windows SAPI 5 voice. Pick the speech output, voice, rate, volume and pitch in F9, Speech settings.

Where things go
- The program goes in the folder you choose in this installer. Per-user install is the default and needs no administrator rights.
- Your settings, saved games, seasons, own sounds, customization files and the log are in your AppData Roaming folder, in Kitchensinc Revival, then NFL Football 4. The installer never changes them, and installing a newer version keeps them.
- Uninstalling removes the whole program folder, closing the game first if it is running. It asks whether to delete your settings, saved games, own sounds and log too. The default is to keep them. Put your own files in the NFL Football 4 folder in AppData, not in the program folder, because the program folder is removed completely.

Windows SmartScreen
This installer is not code-signed, because signing costs money. Windows may say it protected your PC. Choose "More info", then "Run anyway".
