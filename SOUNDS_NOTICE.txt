Notice about the sound files and the license

The MIT License in the file LICENSE covers the new program code, tests and documents written for this game. That code was written from a description of how Jim Kitchen's original program behaves and contains none of his program code.

It does not cover the 26 sound files foot0.wav to foot25.wav in clone\assets\sounds. Those are Jim Kitchen's original wav files from his game "NFL football version 4", copied unchanged (each one checked by its SHA-256 fingerprint against the original). He was blind from birth, made his games free for the blind community, and died in 2015. They are included on the maintainer's own responsibility. If a rights holder asks for them to be removed, they will be removed.

The maintainer, Josh Kennedy, says that Jim Kitchen gave him permission to use the wav files of his games, but the original e-mail is lost, so that permission cannot be shown to anyone.

The 19 stadium, crowd and whistle sounds in clone\assets\sounds_nx are generated by this project's own code. They are new work and are covered by the MIT License.
