The stadium sounds of the N. F. L. rules (SY.2 and SY.3; the whistles, crowd_big_play and crowd_flag_cheer made again on 2026-10-23).
GENERATED by work/s82/make_sfx.py and work/s83b/make_whistles.py: new original work (MIT, with the rest of the program code), not recordings
and not Jim Kitchen's sounds. A file with the same name in the sounds folder of your NFL Football 4 folder in AppData replaces the one here,
and a new number adds one to its family (crowd_cheer2.wav, crowd_cheer3.wav ...): the game draws among them. Your own whistle.wav in that
folder replaces every whistle of the game. Delete your file to get the shipped sound back. F9, Rules, Stadium sounds switches them off.
