## 5. Mechanics (the schedule maker, standings, game clock, score speech, the play engine (5.5 to 5.13), the season engine and the play-offs (5.14 to 5.17))

### 5.1 Schedule maker (`makes`, p_42fa70) [CODE (lifted, S3.3; the constants and the picks checked in the assembly)]
Runs at a new season and when the player rejects the schedule in week 1. All draws are `Int(Rnd * N) + 1` (uniform, D-013).
1. Table of 16 weeks, each with opponent division, opponent team and home flag, all 0. `last` = Empty (counts as 0).
2. The player's division: for each of the three other teams of the division in team order (tn2 = 1 to 4 except the player's), twice (flag 1, then flag 2): draw a week 1 to 16 until it is free and not 1 or 2 weeks away from `last` (so the very first draw cannot give week 1 or 2, because `last` is 0; later draws can); `last` = that week; store (the player's division, the rival's team number, the flag). This puts the six division games in random weeks, never next to the previously placed one.
3. The 10 free weeks in week order: draw a division 1 to 8 until it is not the player's, draw a team 1 to 4, repeat both until that team is not yet on the schedule; draw a flag 1 or 2 until the count of that flag among these 10 games is below 5; store. Result: 8 games with flag 1 and 8 with flag 2, every opponent a different team, each division rival twice, 10 other teams once.
4. Write footbal2.dat: for week 1 to 16 the three values (opponent division, opponent team, flag) (section 12).
The numbers are Single Variants (Int of Rnd, vt 4, checked at 0x42fe0b) in the non-division games and Integer Variants in the division games (the For counters and tn1, vt 2, INFERRED); this only matters for the byte layout of the file (section 12).

### 5.2 Standings (`SayStand`) [CODE (lifted, S3.3; the sort checked in the assembly at 0x42da3b)]

### 5.3 Game clock (`sgc`, F4 and quarter starts) [CODE (lifted, S3.3; constants checked in the assembly)]
Arguments: played = plays played in the quarter, total = plays in this quarter (25 to 35), quarter = 1 to 4 (5 to 10 = over time). Time is made up from the play counter.
```
if played <> oldtime:                      oldtime starts Empty, which equals 0
    oldtime = played;  left = total - played
    pct = Int(left * 100 / total);  spread = 30; low = 15
    if quarter = 2 or quarter > 3:
        W = M2W (quarter 2) | M4W (quarter 4) | M5W (quarter > 4);  M2W, M4W, M5W = Int(Rnd * 3) + 5 = 5 to 7, set by pag
        if left < 5 + W:  CountDown = CountDown + W;  spread = 18; low = 9
        if left <= W:     CountDown = 125 if W = 5, 170 if W = 6, 215 if W = 7;  spread = 10; low = 5
    m = pct * 15 / CountDown;  mins = Int(m);  secs = Int((Int(m * 100) - mins * 100) * 60 / 100)
    draw j = Int(Rnd * spread) - low until 0 <= secs + j <= 59;  secs = secs + j
if quarter = 3 and played = 0:  mins = 15; secs = 0
text = " "; if mins > 0: text = Str$(mins) & " minute" & "s" if mins > 1
if secs > 0: text = text & " " & Str$(secs) & " second" & "s" if secs > 1
say5(text & " remaining in the " & (first, second, third, fourth, over time) & " quarter. ");  clk;  g = "112"
```
CountDown is 100 at the start of every quarter (pag, S3.4) and is saved in football.sav; the same play counter gives the same time again (oldtime), so F4 twice in one play repeats the minutes and seconds. Quirks in section 15. S7.3a (D-039, TESTED, `work/vb_variant_ops_s73a.py`): the numbers of this routine are Variants and the plays of a quarter (`total`, the argument 2) are a SINGLE Variant (`Int(Rnd * 8) + 30` is stored with FSTP float and the type word 4, asm 0x458328 to 0x458338), while the play counter is an Integer Variant; the automation library's VarSub, VarMul, VarDiv, VarAdd and VarInt, which the runtime's `__vbaVar*` helpers call, give a Single for a Single with an Integer or with another Single (only two Integers divided give a Double), so every operation of the chain (left, pct, m, Int(m * 100), the seconds) is a Single operation and its result is rounded to 32 bits before the next one. The game rounds after every operation (`game/vbnum.single`). Over every input the game can give (total 30 to 37, played 0 to total, CountDown 100, 125, 170, 215 and 100 + n x W: 3,588 inputs) the minutes and seconds equal the library's; a Double chain differs in 57 (the first: total 30, played 10: Int(9.9 * 100) is 989 in a Single, so 53 seconds, and 990 in a Double, so 54).

### 5.7 The computer's play choice [CODE (lifted, S3.4; every Rnd and constant checked against the asm, `work/rnd_check_pag_main.txt`)]
- The player on offense: when he takes item 1 to 7 the computer defends with Int(Rnd * 6) + 1 (each defense 1/6), with one exception: a counter of the player's repeats (0 when he chooses a different play than his previous offense play): at the second time in a row the defense matches the play with chance 1/2, from the third time on always. The matching defense: Line Plunge - Goal Line, End Run - Long Run, Screen Pass or Short Pass - Short Pass, Long Pass - Long Pass (field goal and punt: no match). The memory of the previous play is cleared whenever the player defends.
- The player on defense, after he takes defense 1 to 6: x = Int(Rnd * 9) + 2, the play is Int(x / 2): Line Plunge 2/9, End Run 2/9, Screen Pass 2/9, Short Pass 2/9, Long Pass 1/9. Then, in this order:
  a. Last play of a period (plays of the period minus the counter = 1): quarters 1 and 3 or in its own half: the normal rules below; quarter 2: field goal; quarter 4 or overtime: field goal unless the player leads by 4 or more (then the normal rules).
  b. Downs 1 to 3: the drawn play.
  c. Fourth down: punt (7), or field goal (6) when in the player's half with the yard line below 41. Then the kick is replaced by "go for it" (the play Int(Rnd * 4) + 1: Line Plunge, End Run, Screen Pass or Short Pass, never Long Pass) as follows:
     - quarters 1 and 3: kick when in its own half inside its own 40, or when more than 2 yards to go and the yard line above 5; otherwise go when the player leads by more than 3 and the yard line is below 4; otherwise go with chance 1/9.
     - overtime: kick when in its own half with more than 2 yards to go, or in the player's half below the 25; otherwise 1/9.
     - quarters 2 and 4: kick when more than 20 yards to go; in its own half always kick (the intended random test compares with a variable that is never set, Q15); in the player's half go when the score difference is more than 14 either way; then quarter 2: go when fewer than 7 plays are left in the quarter, kick when more than 5 yards to go, otherwise 1/9; quarter 4: kick when the yard line is below 30 and the player does not lead by 4 or more, go when the counter is past 20 and the player leads by less than 8, go when fewer than 7 plays are left, kick when more than 5 yards to go, otherwise 1/9.
- The computer always receives when it wins the toss, returns a kick-off 1 time in 2 (else a touch back), and always kicks the extra point.
