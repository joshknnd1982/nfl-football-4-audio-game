## 1. Speech and pauses

- **G-S1 Asynchronous, synchronous and purged speech.** Status: SETTLED (CODE (lifted say, say2, say3, say4, say5, say6, stopspeech, saykey, inrep) and TESTED). The original speaks through one SpVoice object in three ways. Flags 0 (say, say3, say4, and the bare `Speak " "` calls): returns only when the WHOLE queue of that voice has been spoken, so a synchronous line is also a barrier for every asynchronous line queued before it. Flags 1 (say2, say5, say6, the replay lines of inrep, the key echo, and the bare `Voice.Speak " Hike! ", 1` in the snap count): returns at once; queued lines play one after the other with no pause between them. Flags 2 with "" (stopspeech, called by getkey, getkey2 and the key echo): purge, so the first key at a key wait cuts everything queued. A voice reload is `Set Voice = Nothing` and a new SpVoice; releasing a speaking voice stops its speech (TESTED: releasing at once left 0 percent of a long text rendered, after 0.3 s 72 percent, after 1.0 s 100 percent because a memory stream renders faster than real time). say3 guards its reload with a synchronous `Speak " "` on the old voice (a barrier, so nothing is pending when it releases); the announcer's speakers (say, say2, say4, say5, inrep) reload only after say3 (synchronous, nothing pending) or after the F11 and F12 menus. The one unguarded case is " Hike! ": spoken asynchronously in the quarterback's voice, then PlayHike and the 0.6 s ramp c2dh (S5.2 correction: 0.7 s, 7 steps of 0.1 s from wavol down to -600 + wavol), then say4 in the announcer's voice; when the two voice numbers differ and "Hike!" is still speaking after the ramp, the original cuts its tail (new quirk Q52). Clone: one first-in-first-out speech queue for all roles; asynchronous = queue and return; synchronous = queue and wait until the queue is empty (the kit's say_async and say); stop = purge the queue (the first key at a key wait, F1 to F4 handling as spoken in 4.15); nothing is ever cut by a voice change (Q52 not reproduced: "Hike!" plays to its end and the next line waits behind it). Keys are not delivered while a synchronous line is spoken (G-K1). Closes: S7.1 (speech layer test: order, barrier, purge), S8.1.

- **G-S2 Pauses (dy and rady).** Status: DEFAULT for the clone's model; the facts are SETTLED (CODE (lifted), all 64 procedures, `work\pause_table_s43.txt`). 68 pause sites: 28 dy (fixed seconds) and 40 rady (random). 49 of them follow a synchronous say (the pause is then an audible gap: nothing speaks), 6 follow an asynchronous say (the pause runs while that speech plays, no gap, it only delays the next statement: the three in SayScedual between the schedule lines, one in readins, two in pag after the replay and after the quarter statistics), 6 follow a sound or a ramp (3 ramps, 3 waited sounds), and 7 are inside helpers (the four crowd ramps' 0.1 s steps, PlayTackle's 2.5 s, inrep's 0.2 s, and one before a say4 in finplay17). dy waits exactly its seconds; rady waits Int(Rnd * 30) / (max(vr, 1) * 25) seconds: 0 to 1.16 s, mean 0.58 s at rate 1 or below, 0.12 s at rate 5, 0.06 s at rate 10. A snap count has about 4.4 rady (1 after "hut 1", 1/7 after "hut 2", 4/5 after the first call, 1.5 in the "hut" loop, 1 before "Hike!"): mean 2.6 s at rate 1 or below, 0.5 s at rate 5, plus dy(0.2) after the crowd ramp. Both loops pump keys and are not ended by a key. Clone: the kit's `KeyInput.dy` (monotonic clock, pump every millisecond) with the queued speech playing meanwhile; rady is the same formula with the game's rate counter (G-S4); the pauses are not scaled or shortened (the player's pace control is the rate, as in the original). Not reproduced: two rady calls in the same 15 ms clock tick draw the same value because both reseed from Timer (inaudible). Closes: S7.4 (rig test: the virtual-clock length of a snap count, a kick-off, a touchdown), S8.2 (does the pace feel right; if a pause scale is wanted it is one factor on dy and rady).

- **G-S3 Str$ and doubled spaces.** Status: SETTLED (TESTED, replaces the remark in D-025). Str$ puts a space before every non-negative number, so " game number " & Str$(1) is " game number  1" with two spaces (also " This is week  7 of ...", " Standings after week number  3", " it's your ball on my  30yard line"; S5.2 correction: not the standings heading, which trims the number: " Standings after week number 3", SayStand 0x42d62d). Rendering the doubled and the single space forms to memory with 6 different voices of the owner's 1,811 (7 runs: the first voice ran twice) (`work\sapi_spaces_test_s43.txt`): the audio length was identical in all 56 pairs (the shared probe, run again with each voice once: 48 of 48, `work\sapi_spaces_test_s43_rerun.txt`); the samples differed only as much as the same text differs from itself (the engines add run-to-run noise: control), and one voice (BeSTspeech) was byte identical. So there is no audible pause, in SAPI voices; NVDA and JAWS collapse white space as well (INFERRED, S8.2 listens). Clone: build every text exactly as the original does (a `vb_str` helper: leading space for numbers 0 or more, "-" for negatives, Trim$ where the original trims) and send it unchanged; conformance tests compare the exact strings; the speech log keeps them. - **G-S5 The key echo rate.** Status: SETTLED (S5.2, TESTED; was DEFAULT). The original speaks a typed character at rate vr + 5, then restores vr. Clone: the SAPI backend renders the echo at vr + 5 (clamped to 10) when the layer takes a per-call rate, else at the normal rate; NVDA and JAWS at their own rate. S5.2 (audit finding A-27, TESTED with `games\tools\sapi_probes\sapi_echo_rate_test.py`, output `work\sapi_echo_rate_test_s52.txt`): saykey sets Rate = vr + 5, purges, queues the character ASYNCHRONOUSLY and sets Rate = vr at once, and SAPI renders the queued text at the restored rate: with the default voice, Microsoft David and Microsoft Zira, a one-character echo and a sentence rendered through that sequence were byte for byte as long as the same text at rate vr (43,116 bytes against 24,740 at vr + 5 for one character with the default voice), and on the sound device at volume 0 a sentence took 4.33 s against 4.34 s at vr and 2.51 s at vr + 5. Clone (replaces the default above): the echo is spoken at the normal rate in every backend (SAPI at vr, NVDA and JAWS at their own rate); the echo test checks that no rate step is applied. Closes: S8.1 (echo test), nothing else.

## 2. Keys and input

- **G-K1 Keys typed while the program is busy.** Status: SETTLED (TESTED, CODE (lifted clk, getkey, getkey2, dy, rady, Form_KeyDown) and the kit). The original runs Form_KeyDown only inside DoEvents, which dy, rady, the wait loops, the waited sounds and clk (twice) call. A synchronous Speak does not pump messages: a 24.1 s call let 0 timer messages and 0 posted messages through and delivered all of them at the next pump (`work\sapi_sync_pump_test_s43.txt`). So keys typed during narration wait in the Windows queue; the next pump (normally the first DoEvents of clk, which has just set g to "") delivers them in order, each overwriting g and g2, so only the LAST one survives, and clk then clears g2 but not g: the next key wait ends at once with the last key's code and no character. Menus that read the code (Enter, Up, Down, F1 to F4, Esc) act on it (Enter typed during narration chooses item 1 of the next play menu: new quirk Q54); menus that read the character (digits, y, n) treat it as any other key. Keys typed during pauses and waited sounds are delivered at once: global keys (F5 to F8, F11, F12, Ctrl, which run their menu inside the pump) act, any other key is cleared by the next clk. Clone: the kit's `KeyInput` unchanged (the starter kit implements exactly this; its comment says so); the clone's added menus use `read_key`, which never discards a key. Q54 is kept. Closes: S8.1 (type-ahead during a synchronous line, a pause and a waited sound), S8.2.

- **G-K2 Keys for the clone's added functions.** Status: DEFAULT. F9 is the one function key the original does not use (it gave the character "x" as an invalid key). F9 opens one added-functions menu, a global key like F5 to F12 (D-018), with the items speech settings (mode, voice per role, rate), sounds (7.7: open folder, reload, export, reset, added sounds, volumes), customize (7.9: open folder, reload names, check files, reset) and about (0.5). Up, Down, digits and Enter choose, Escape leaves, and the menu ends like the other global menus (F1 handling, D-018). F1 to F4 stay what the original makes them. Closes: S7.4.

- **G-K3 Modifier keys.** Status: DEFAULT. In the original every key counts, also Shift, Ctrl (which starts the rate menu), Insert and CapsLock; at a "press any key" wait any of them ends the wait. Clone: the same (the kit's getkey sees every key; only the added menus skip modifier keys). Watch item for S8.2: if NVDA or JAWS passes its own modifier keys (Insert, CapsLock) on to the window and that restarts a menu, add VK_INSERT and VK_CAPITAL to a small ignore set in the key waits (one line). ## 3. Sound

- **G-A1 Sound slots.** Status: SETTLED (CODE (lifted), WAV_EVENT_MAP 1 and 4). The original has seven buffer fields: one-shot (intro, play, play2, playcel, ChangeVoice), crowd 1, crowd 2, hike, ambient, tackle (PlayTackle: foot4, foot5, foot6 and foot11) and play noise. Loading a sound into a field replaces, and so cuts, whatever the field was playing. PlayTackle does not wait for its sound (4.3 s, 3.1 s, 2.9 s and 1.95 s long) but only 2.5 s, so a second PlayTackle within the first sound's length cuts it; the hike (foot3, 3.1 s) restarts from 0 at every snap. Clone: the mixer has the same seven slots; starting a sound in a slot stops that slot's current sound; different slots mix; plus ONE extra channel that is not an original slot and is never used in play (the previews of the F9 sounds menu and the exit beep, SOUND_DESIGN 6; S4.4). Closes: S7.2 (slot test with the mixer log), S8.1.

- **G-A2 The play noise loop.** Status: DEFAULT. foot14 loops from PlayNoise (4 starts: PlayHike and three kick starts) until a PlayTackle (18 sites in pag) stops it. A path that reached a menu or a key wait with the loop still running would play noise under the menu in the original. Clone: the same logic plus a safety net: at the start of every key wait and quarter break the loop is stopped if it still runs, and the rig test counts: over 10,000 simulated plays no foot14 loop runs during any key wait (if the faithful logic fails the test, that path is a quirk of the original and is decided then). S5.2 (audit finding A-03, CODE (lifted)): one such path exists in the original: the field goal from the player's own half (" is way to short", pag 0x4424df to 0x442626) starts foot14 after kicksnap and calls no PlayTackle, so the loop runs under the possession sentence, the banter, the down sentence and the next play menu until the next PlayTackle (the end of the next play, a quarter end or a two-minute warning); quirk Q57, not reproduced (D-034): the clone stops the loop when " is way to short" is spoken, with no other sound, and the safety net stays as written. The rig test must include this path with the player's own choices (the computer never kicks from its own half, 5.7). Closes: S8.1.

## 5. Data, names and randomness

- **G-D1 Redraw loops.** Status: DEFAULT. The original has unbounded "draw again while ..." loops (the surname pool, the schedule maker, the score table). With the original data none can run forever (1,708 names for 68 draws, 32 teams for 10 slots, 32 scores), but a customized pool or a damaged file might. Clone: every such loop is bounded (10,000 tries), then takes the first valid candidate by scanning, and logs it. Closes: S8.1 (fuzz test).

- **G-D2 Random numbers.** Status: DEFAULT (D-013 stands). One generator from the system clock behind one function `rnd(n)` (= Int(Rnd * n) + 1 in the original's terms); every draw keeps the range of its lifted line. Tests use a scripted generator for forced situations (every row of the outcome table, each kick result, each branch of the fourth-down rules, each play-off bracket) and a seeded generator for distribution checks (chi-square) of the main draws: coin toss, defense choice, the computer's offense choice, the outcome yards, sack, completion and interception chances, the 1/50 fumble, field goals, punt distance, kick-off returns, plays per quarter (30 to 37), the score table. Thresholds are written when the tests are. Closes: S8.1.
