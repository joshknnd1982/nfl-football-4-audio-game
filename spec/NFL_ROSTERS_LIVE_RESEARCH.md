# NFL_ROSTERS_LIVE_RESEARCH (nfl4 only): SR.1, the audit and the research plan for the real rosters (phase PR, rule 0.32, NX-L1 to NX-L10; D-152 to D-155)

Written in session SR.1 (2026-10-25). Facts only (names, teams, positions, numbers). Claude is not a lawyer. Everything below was read as RAW data by scripts in `work\pr\` (not tracked: `audit_rosters.py`, `fetch_raw.py`, `parse_team_pages.py`; the saved pages and feeds are in `work\pr\raw\`, the reports are `work\pr\audit_rosters_report.txt` and `work\pr\team_pages_report.txt`). ## 1. The audit of what the game has (work\pr\audit_rosters.py)

Part 1, rosters.txt itself (VERSION as of 2026-10-24; the coaches file says 2026-10-22): 1,678 active men and 545 practice squad men for 32 teams. Every team has a quarterback, a kicker, a punter and a long snapper, every name is once on a team. Six teams have a practice squad of 18 or 19 (T02, T04, T15, T18, T22, T28); the 2026 limit is 16 plus one International Pathway player, 17 (section 4), so those squads hold at least one man who is not on a squad (SR.2 settles them). Position words in the file are the original's groups: QB RB FB WR TE DL LB CB S K P OL LS and RET lines.

Part 2, a NEW franchise built with this tree (0.16.1, the bench on), three seeds and three player teams, the same numbers each time (no draw decides it): 1,678 real men in the file, 1,695 players in the league, 69 real men left off, 86 made-up men on teams, 164 real men stored at another position than the file gives: 87 tight ends stored as wide receivers, 32 wide receivers and 30 tight ends and 2 cornerbacks stored as "RET" (the returner loses his position), 13 fullbacks stored as running backs. The league has no tackle, guard, center, end, tackle, outside or middle linebacker, free or strong safety: its positions are the original's. The Bears (T17): all three quarterbacks (Caleb Williams, Case Keenum, Tyson Bagent); missing Jayden Loving, Shemar Turner (defensive linemen) and Luke Newman (a lineman); made up George Robertson, Tyler Svendsen, Trent Brokenborough; Colston Loveland and Sam Roush (tight ends) stored as wide receivers, Zavion Thomas and Clark Phillips III stored as "RET". This confirms section 21.1 of the spec for all 32 teams.

Part 3, an OLD league (format 16 and older, or a build older than 0.16.0: places only, no bench): 1,088 players for 1,678 real men, 676 real men left off, EXACTLY ONE quarterback on every team, the Bears' only Caleb Williams. That is "the Bears don't have one quarterback" in the sense that matters (no second quarterback at all; a team that loses its starter has a made-up stand-in): reproduced. Q-073 (the tester's version) stays owner-needed and is not blocking.

## 2. rosters.txt against the teams' own pages (work\pr\team_pages_report.txt)

Method: the raw HTML of the roster page and the depth chart page of five teams, read on 2026-10-25 about a day after the file (Bears, Dolphins, Ravens, Falcons, Rams: a 4-3, a 3-4 and mixed fronts; the three teams with the file's strangest rows). The page has four tables: Active, Reserve/Injured (also "Reserve/Injured; Designated for Return", "Physically Unable to Perform", "Non-Football Injury", "Suspended by Commissioner"), Practice Squad, Practice Squad/Injured.

- Bears and Rams: the file equals the page exactly (53 and 52 active, 17 and 16 on the squad, every number the same). The Rams page does list Aaron Donald on its Active table (so the row that looked wrong in 2026-10-24's summary was right; the raw page proves it) and Davante Adams under Reserve/Injured, and Myles Garrett is on the page too.
- Dolphins: the page has Kyle Louis (a linebacker) active and the file has no Kyle Louis; the file has the practice squad kicker Zane Gonzalez as the active kicker (SX.11's rule, the page has no active kicker). Ravens: the page has Kyle Hergel active (the file: squad), Tavius Robinson is on the page's injured list (the file: active). Falcons: the page has A.J. Terrell Jr. (their first cornerback) and Drake Jackson active (the file has neither; "Falcons activate A.J. Terrell" is a headline on the same page) and Michael Jerrell on a reserve list.
- So 5 of 32 teams give 7 differences of about 290 names: some are real moves of the day after the file (an activation), a summary that dropped a row cannot be excluded, and the file's team-by-team practice squads are off by one to three men. The method of 2026-10-24 (page summaries) is NOT a source: SR.2 rebuilds the file from raw data, then these five teams are the first hand-check.
- The page labels positions COARSELY and differently per team: Bears QB RB WR TE OL DL LB DB K P LS; Dolphins C G G/T T EDGE DL ILB LB S CB; Ravens C G G/T T NT DL ILB OLB S CB; Falcons C/G OL OT T DE DT DL ILB LB DB CB S; Rams OL DE NT OLB ILB CB S. Their depth charts have their own rows (Bears: DL DL DL DL LB LB S S DB CB CB KO H KR PR; Ravens: DE DT NT RUSH SAM WLB MLB SS FS). One Rams row lost its number cell (a parser slip: the parser must keep empty cells).

## 3. Madden's positions, from EA's own pages (Q-074, NX-L2)

EA's Madden NFL 27 ratings pages (https://www.ea.com/games/madden-nfl/ratings and the position pages, read as raw HTML on 2026-10-25, 22 position links) list exactly these positions: QB, HB, FB, WR, TE, LT, LG, C, RG, RT, LEDG, REDG, DT, SAM (strong-side "sam backer"), MIKE ("mike backer"), WILL ("weak backer"), CB, FS, SS, K, P, LS. SRC-OFFICIAL (EA). This is NOT the list the playbook had assumed: Madden NFL 27 has no LE, RE, LOLB, MLB or ROLB; its defense is two edge men (left, right), the tackle, and three backers by their role. The pages give no depth-chart roles; kick returner and punt returner stay depth-chart roles (KR and PR rows exist in every team's chart, section 5) and are tagged UNVERIFIED as Madden's. Only structure is used: no rating, no text, no art of EA's (Q-074 answered: the ratings stay the game's own).

## 4. Roster limits of 2026 (SRC-SECONDARY, to confirm from operations.nfl.com in SR.2)

53 players on the active roster from the cut-down (2026-08-30) on, injured reserve and the other reserve lists do not count; 48 can be active on a game day (a less reliable site says 46); practice squad 16, plus a 17th if he is from the International Pathway Program. The Dolphins page shows 53 active plus one "Active/Physically Unable to Perform"; the Ravens and Rams show 52 (a short roster mid-week is real). NX-L8's checks are therefore: active 46 to 53 (as in the spec), squad at most 17, reserve lists apart.

Findings that decide the plan: (1) no source is both official, raw, granular and free to read by script: the official pages are the best truth and their terms bar the script; (2) the aggregators (nflverse, Sleeper, ESPN) are all fed from the same few providers, so their agreement is not independent proof; (3) every pair of sources disagrees by a few men a team, so the script's report of differences and a web search per difference (rule 0.8, NX-L1) is the real method.

## 6. Decisions of SR.1 (spec\DECISIONS D-152 to D-155)

- **D-152 The position list.** The game's positions are Madden NFL 27's 22 (section 3): QB, HB, FB, WR, TE, LT, LG, C, RG, RT, LEDG, REDG, DT, SAM, MIKE, WILL, CB, FS, SS, K, P, LS, with KR and PR as depth-chart roles. NX-L2 and playbook 7.23 are corrected (they named LE, RE, LOLB, MLB, ROLB). The file's new position words are these codes; the speech says them in full words (halfback, left tackle, left edge, tackle, strong-side backer, ...), SR.6.
- **D-153 The mapping from the sources' labels** (decided now for the clear cases; the ambiguous ones are SR.3's, with the data named, tag UNVERIFIED): RB and HB are HB; FB is FB; SWR LWR RWR WR are WR; the offensive line comes from the chart rows LT LG C RG RT; a lineman no row names is placed by the roster word (T and OT: LT or RT by the side of the first free row, G and OG: LG or RG, C: C; G/T and C/G: the row the chart names, else his first word). In a "Base 4-3 D" chart LDE and RDE are LEDG and REDG, LDT and RDT are DT, WLB MLB SLB are WILL MIKE SAM, LCB RCB NB are CB, FS SS are FS SS. In a "Base 3-4 D" chart NT is DT, LDE and RDE are DT (the ends of a 3-4 line are tackles by weight: UNVERIFIED), the outside backers WLB and SLB (who rush) are LEDG and REDG (UNVERIFIED), LILB and RILB are MIKE and WILL. A man the roster word calls EDGE or DE and no row names is an edge man; DL or DT is DT; ILB or MLB is MIKE; OLB is WILL or SAM by the free row; DB goes to CB unless a row says FS or SS. The returner is a role of the man's own position, never a position (KR and PR rows, kicker KO and holder H are not positions). The edge/tackle/backer calls of the 3-4 fronts are checked in SR.3 against the commercial game's team charts as far as EA's pages show them and against two sources; where they cannot be settled the choice is the mapping above, recorded.
- **D-154 The sources of the script and of the research.** The script update_rosters.py (SR.2) reads: nflverse's rosters CSV (membership, status, number, position word) and the newest snapshot of nflverse's depth chart (positions, order, roles) as the primary, Sleeper as the second source for the position word and the order, with the differences listed; it never reads the teams' sites (terms 1.3). The official team pages are read by hand-sized sample after each build (a fixed set of teams, at most 6, never all 32 in a run), and every disagreement is settled by a web search and recorded; this keeps rule 0.32's "official sources first" as far as the terms allow and says plainly where it does not reach. The in-game update downloads only the maintainer's checked file from the repository (as the calendars do); the second item of NX-L9 ("pull from the sources inside the game") is DROPPED for the player's copy: Sleeper's "once a day at most" and the NFL's terms make a pull by every player wrong, and the pull stays a maintainer tool. Reversible: Q-075.
- **D-155 The checks.** Limits of section 4. The script also checks: every status word known, no man on two teams, a man on a reserve list or the squad is not in the active set, the quarterback, kicker, punter and long snapper of NX-L8, and reports every change since the last file. A pull never replaces a good file.
